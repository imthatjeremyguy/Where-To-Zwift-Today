Help

Quick answers about how Where to Zwift Today works.

How does the spin pick a route?

It picks at random from the routes in today's worlds that match your Spin settings: sport, route badges, terrain, route type, distance and climbing. Tap world chips to spin from only those worlds. Event-only routes and routes Zwift hasn't released yet are never picked. Your last 10 picks on this device are skipped too, so daily spins stay varied (in a small pool, at most half of it is held back).

Why do the worlds change?

Watopia is always available. Two guest worlds rotate on Zwift's schedule, changing at midnight US Eastern (4am UTC in summer, 5am in winter). The site reads Zwift's own schedule every day. If Zwift hasn't published the next part of the schedule yet, spins come from Watopia only until it does.

What do Flat, Rolling and Hilly mean?

They come from climbing per kilometer on the route itself, not counting the lead-in: Flat is under 5 m/km, Rolling is 5 to 10 m/km, and Hilly is 10 m/km or more. Loop and Point to point come from Zwift's own data: Loop routes finish where they start and can be ridden in laps.

Do the distance and climbing filters include the lead-in?

Yes. Filters use the total you actually ride: the route plus its lead-in from the start pen. The result card shows the route, the lead-in and the total separately.

How do completed routes work?

On the Completed routes tab, tick the routes you have the badge for, or press Mark as done on a spun route. Then set Route badges to "Not done yet" and spins will skip them. Ride and Run are tracked separately, like Zwift's badges. The site can't read your Zwift account, so ticks are entered by you.

Where is my progress saved, and how do I move it to another device?

Progress and settings are saved in your browser on this device only. There are no accounts. To move progress, press "Copy my progress link" and open that link on the other device; it asks before replacing anything. Bookmarking the link also works as a backup. Clearing your browser's site data or using private browsing will lose progress that hasn't been saved as a link.

What are Route of the Week and Climb of the Week?

Zwift features one route and one climb each week, with bonus XP or Drops for completing them. They switch every Monday at noon US Eastern. The Calendar tab shows the current pair and the upcoming weeks.

What are the climb portals on the Calendar?

Watopia's climb portal features a different climb each day. France's portal has a climb of the month, which you can only ride on days France is a guest world, so the Calendar shows the next France day. Portal climbs are not routes, so they never come up in a spin.

Was AI used to build this site?

Yes. Where to Zwift Today was built with the help of Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding assistant. I decided what the site should do and how it should look, and reviewed and checked each change as it was built; the AI wrote most of the code and its automated tests. The site itself doesn't use AI: spins are ordinary random picks, and all route and schedule data comes from Zwift and Zwift Insider.

Where does the data come from?

The world schedule, routes and climb portal rotation come from Zwift's public game files. Route and Climb of the Week come from Zwift Insider's Ride of the Week calendar, and route pages link to Zwift Insider. Everything refreshes automatically every day, so new routes appear on their own.

Something looks wrong. How do I report it?

Please open an issue on GitHub with what you saw and the date. This site isn't affiliated with Zwift, so for problems in the game itself, contact Zwift support.
